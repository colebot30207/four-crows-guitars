Guitar photos go here.

1. Drop the originals into assets/photos/originals/ named:
     hero.jpg            (the big hero photo)
     guitar-01.jpg ... guitar-10.jpg
2. Run:  python3 tools/optimize-photos.py
3. Edit the guitar names in index.html (search for PLACEHOLDER).

The site shows "Photo coming soon" tiles until the .webp files exist.
